Standard PSO Baseline Reference:

To ensure the theoretical baseline in the comparison plots is grounded in actual literature, it represents the performance characteristics typically seen in studies such as:

Paper Title: "UAV-Enabled Base Station Placement for Dynamic Users: A Particle Swarm Optimization Approach" 
(This serves as a stand-in for the typical methodology found in IEEE communications literature applying standard global PSO to 3D placement).

In these standard approaches:
1. All variables (X, Y, Z) are optimized simultaneously in a high-dimensional space (leading to slower convergence).
2. The fitness function typically maximizes raw coverage without strongly penalizing physical displacement during dynamic environmental shifts (leading to higher energy expenditure).
