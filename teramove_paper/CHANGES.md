# TeraMove paper — corrections

`teramove_paper/main.pdf` is the corrected version of `THZ_Placement-2.pdf`.

The repository's `main.tex` ended after the method section, so the source was rebuilt.
Sections I–IV come from `main.tex`. Sections V–VII and the references were reconstructed
from `THZ_Placement-2.pdf` and then corrected as listed below.

Every number in Sections V–VII is a macro in `numbers.tex`, generated by
`teramove_eval/make_assets.py` from `teramove_eval/results/*.json`.

## 1. Simulator fixes (`chsr_sim/`)

| # | Defect | Effect | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Azimuth convention.** The MILP gives azimuths as compass bearings ("from North"), but `physics.py` compared them with math angles (0° = +x, counter-clockwise). | APs listed at 135° pointed out of the building. Empty-floor coverage of the MILP layout was 43.9 % instead of 60.8 %. | `config.AZIMUTH_COMPASS = True`; `physics.py` computes compass bearings. `False` reproduces the old behaviour. |
| 2 | **Figs. 5–6 (PSO/GA) and Fig. 2 (tier gains) were hard-coded numbers.** | Not simulation output. | Real standard-PSO and GA baselines (`chsr_sim/baselines.py`), same fitness, same budget, 30 seeds. Every figure is now generated from results. |
| 3 | Absorption `K_F = 0.05` m⁻¹ (217 dB/km). | About 33× too much loss. | 6.68 dB/km (ITU-R P.676 via ITU-Rpy) → `K_F = 1.54e-3` m⁻¹. |
| 4 | `CANDIDATE_Z = arange(2.0, 3.2, 0.2)` also contained 3.2 m, above the 3 m ceiling. | Rays could pass over 3 m walls. | Heights 2.0–3.0 m, as the paper states. |
| 5 | `decode_routers` clipped positions to [0, 31] × [0, 19]. | APs mounted at y = 19.5 m were moved 0.5 m even in "zero-movement" Tier 2. | Bounds are the walls minus a 0.25 m margin. |
| 6 | The swarm never contained the current configuration. | A tier could return a worse layout than its input. In a test, Tier 2 lowered coverage from 98.0 % to 95.3 %. | Particle 0 is the incumbent (`include_current=True`). |
| 7 | The static-data cache grew without bound for continuous Tier-4 positions. | About 5.6 GB at 200 × 200. | Cache capped at 20 000 entries. |
| 8 | The swarm size in the paper (S = 50, T = 20) differed from the code (200 × 200). | Inconsistent setup. | `config.py` now uses S = 50, T = 20, the values the paper states. |
| 9 | **Cache invalidated by `id(human_density)`.** CPython reuses the address of a freed array, so a new crowd map can inherit the previous map's path losses. | Results depend on memory-allocation history and are not reproducible. Found when a stored run could not be reproduced. This affected the original TeraMove runs too. | Caches are keyed by array content (BLAKE2 hash) and wall contents. |

Regression tests for fixes 1, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 9 are in `tests/test_legacy_fixes.py`. After fix 9, all experiments were re-run, and stored runs reproduce bit-for-bit.

## 2. Text fixes

* **Noise floor.** −94 dBm in Table II, −64 dBm in the code. The correct value for 10 GHz and
  a 10 dB noise figure is −64 dBm. Table II now gives −64 dBm and its derivation.
* **Crowd model.** The text said "empirical pedestrian density model". The code generates
  synthetic circular clusters, and t0/t1/t2 are independent draws. The paper now
  describes exactly that.
* **Fitness weights.** The method section gave β = 0.15, but the paper adopted
  configuration D (β = 0.25). The text now states configuration D.
* **Table IV.** Configuration D's displacement was 69.1 m in the table but 42.2 m in the
  text and in `results_D_dynamic.txt`. Table IV is now regenerated.
* **Reference [7].** The text said "Alameh et al.", but [7] is by Liu, Jian, Sivakumar and
  Blough. The text is corrected.
* **References [2] and [4].** The two entries were garbled together. Both are repaired.
* **MILP section.** "The linearized form of Eq." was missing its equation reference and
  verb. Fixed.
* **Fig. 1.** It was the MILP's own SINR map, computed under the MILP's channel model
  (10 dBm, 24 dBi). It is now the simulator's map of the commissioned layout. The text
  explains the commissioning step (positions unchanged; beams and heights tuned on the
  empty floor, 61.9 % → 93.3 %).
* **Statistics.** All results are means over 30 runs with 95 % CIs and Wilcoxon tests.
  The old paper claimed 30 Monte Carlo runs that did not exist.

## 3. Results: old vs corrected

All corrected values are means over 30 runs; p-values are Wilcoxon signed-rank tests.

| Quantity | Old paper | Corrected |
|---|---|---|
| Coverage at t0 before adaptation | 16.6 % | 62.8 % |
| Coverage after adaptation, t0 / t1 / t2 | 97.1 / 95.6 / not given | 94.8 / 95.6 / 92.1 % |
| Tier gains (Table III) | +26.5 / +3.5 / +3.3 pp from 63.6 % | +4.4 / +5.6 / +4.0 pp from 82.9 % |
| Share of the gain from Tier 2 | "over 79 %" | 31.4 % |
| Hungarian vs nearest neighbour | −8.6 %, 4.1 m | −6.0 %, 0.5 m, not significant (p =0.18) |
| Total displacement t0–t2, HPSO vs standard PSO | 42.2 m vs "over 450 m" (−90.6 %) | 16.0 m vs 86.9 m (−81.6 %); GA 182.3 m (−91.2 %) |
| Average coverage t0–t2, HPSO / PSO / GA | — | 94.2 / 95.3 (p =0.03) / 94.4 % |
| Steps meeting the 90 % target, HPSO / PSO / GA | — | 97.8 / 90.0 / 88.9 % |
| Dense-crowd comparison | HPSO > 88 % vs PSO ≈ 74 % (fabricated) | On 75 triggered events: HPSO 94.6 %, PSO 97.9 % (p <0.001), GA 95.5 %; displacement 6.9 / 32.9 / 45.7 m |
| Convergence | "HPSO fastest, GA slowest" (fabricated) | Standard PSO fastest; final fitness PSO 1.429, HPSO 1.405, GA 1.360 |
| Table IV | D best (95.2 %, 42.2 / 69.1 m) | All 93.9–94.6 %; no configuration differs significantly from D (p ≥ 0.16) |

**What changes in the conclusions:**
* The 16.6 % "crowd collapse" was mostly caused by the mis-oriented APs (fix 1).
* HPSO does not converge faster than a standard PSO. On single triggered events, the PSO
  also reaches higher coverage.
* HPSO's real strength is its far lower physical movement. Over the multi-step sequence,
  it also meets the coverage target most reliably.

The abstract, introduction and conclusion now say this.

## 4. Not re-verified

The MILP results quoted in Sec. V-A were not re-run, as instructed:
* 14 APs for 100 % coverage under the MILP's model;
* 9 → 10 APs for SINR_th 1 → 5 dB;
* 10 vs 15 APs for wide vs narrow beams.

They are now explicitly attributed to the MILP study. The old paper's claim of a "20 %
higher peak SINR than uniform height" was removed, because nothing in the repository
supports it.
